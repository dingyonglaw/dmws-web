🎮 Buckle Up!?

Hold onto your keyboards, folks, because DMIS just leveled up BIG TIME! 🎉 Say hello to the DMIS Arcade, our shiny new corner of the internet where learning meets games party! 🕹️

It all started when daddy whipped up a Minecraft Preposition Adventure to make learning prepositions less “ugh” and more “YEAH!”. Ee Jin loved it so much she said, “Dad, let’s make a another one with Pokemon. Quagsire stolen Ash GS ball ...” So, we teamed up, and BOOM—Pokémon Preposition Battle was born! 🔥

Now, we’re hooked! We want to crank out more games that make learning sneakily fun. Dive into the DMIS Arcade, try out our games, and let us know which one makes you snort-laugh the loudest.
