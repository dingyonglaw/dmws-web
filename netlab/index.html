DMIS Net Lab

Pick a shape. Fold it flat. Find the nets that work.

A net is a 3D shape cut open and laid flat. Each shape has a short lesson, then a Net Lab full of right and wrong nets to test.

  1. Watch it unfold. See exactly which flap goes where.
  2. Learn the rules. Each rule comes with a good net and a broken one.
  3. Guess, then fold. Say yes or no first, then fold to check.

Fold it in your head

See a net, decide if it folds, then watch the answer. No touching the shape until you choose!
